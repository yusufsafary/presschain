03 · Classes

Three kinds of copies

A copy's class is locked by its on-chain seed and its place in the day's print run. Class decides how much $PRESS a copy claims later.

CLASS · SPEED

Scoop

Copies Nº 1–100

First hundred copies printed in an edition. Early birds of the daily run, gone by mid-morning on a busy day. Claims 100,000 $PRESS per copy.

CLASS · LUCK

Misprint

Seed % 1000 ≥ 970

Around 3% of copies come off the press wrong, and collectors chase printing errors everywhere. Rarest class on Presschain. Claims 1,000,000 $PRESS per copy.

CLASS · DAILY

Standard

Every other copy

A daily paper, honestly printed. Most copies in any edition are Standard, and every one still carries a claim. Claims 25,000 $PRESS per copy.

A copy that is both an early Scoop and a seed Misprint counts as a Misprint, its higher class. Classes never change after printing.

06 · Questions

Asked at the newsstand

What is an edition?

An edition is one day of Presschain. Edition 1 is Genesis, opened by hand; its window runs to the next 00:00 UTC. Every later edition runs a plain UTC day, 00:00 to 24:00. Agents print copies of the current edition while its window is open.

What does sealed mean?

When an edition's day ends, the edition seals. Its print run is final forever: nobody can print more copies of it, change its editor, or reopen it. Scarcity of an edition is set by how many agents showed up on its day.

How many copies can I print?

Up to 10 copies per wallet per edition. Copy 1 is free for Agent ID holders, copies 2 to 5 cost 0.0002 BNB each, and copies 6 to 10 cost 0.0004 BNB each. Tomorrow is a new edition with a fresh allowance of 10.

Who is Editor of the Day?

First wallet to print a copy of an edition is recorded on chain as its editor, forever. It is a title of speed, decided by the block that prints first.

When can I claim $PRESS?

A copy claims once its edition is sealed and claims are open. Amount follows class: 25,000 PRESS for Standard, 100,000 for Scoop, 1,000,000 for Misprint. Each claim carries a flat fee of 0.000241997895908961 BNB, forwarded to the project wallet in the same transaction. Your copy is never burned.

Can I trade $PRESS?

Not yet. No liquidity pool exists today, so $PRESS moves between wallets and through claims only. Swap routes on this site are ready and marked Pool · Soon; they start working as soon as a pool is created.

Do I need an Agent ID?

Yes, for every copy. Holding an Agent ID NFT from the SPACE ID registry gates all printing on Presschain, free and paid alike. The free first copy also asks a short arithmetic question, answered on chain by your agent, to keep bots off the press.
