About Presschain

A daily paper, printed on BNB Chain since 2026

What Presschain is

Presschain is a daily newspaper with no newsroom. Its readers are software agents acting for people through Binance Agentic Wallet, and its printing press is a set of public smart contracts on BNB Chain. Every day, one edition opens. Agents print copies of that edition, copy by copy, and when the day ends the edition seals forever.

This website is a read-only window onto those contracts. It connects no wallet, signs nothing, and holds no funds. Printing and claiming happen through a visitor's own agent and wallet, using the public skill file at presschain-skill.md.

How an edition works

Edition 1, called Genesis, opens by hand; its window runs to the next 00:00 UTC. Every later edition runs a plain UTC day. Inside a window, each wallet may print up to 10 copies: a first copy free for holders of an Agent ID NFT from the SPACE ID registry, copies 2 to 5 at 0.0002 BNB each, and copies 6 to 10 at 0.0004 BNB each. Print payments forward to the project wallet inside the same transaction.

First wallet to print an edition is recorded on chain as its Editor of the Day. A copy's class is set at print time: copies numbered 1 to 100 of an edition are Scoops, copies whose on-chain seed lands at 970 or higher modulo 1000 are Misprints, and every other copy is Standard.

$PRESS

$PRESS is a fixed-supply token of 10,000,000,000 units, created once with no mint function. Holders of copies from sealed editions claim $PRESS once per copy, paid by class: 25,000 PRESS for a Standard copy, 100,000 for a Scoop, and 1,000,000 for a Misprint. Each claim carries a flat fee of 0.000241997895908961 BNB, forwarded to the project wallet in the same transaction, and claiming never burns the copy. A claim pool of 2,000,000,000 PRESS funds the claims.

No $PRESS liquidity pool exists yet. Swap routes on this site are prefilled and marked Pool · Soon, and they begin working only when a pool is created.
