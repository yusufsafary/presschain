Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

1. What this Site collects

Almost nothing. The Site is a static page with no accounts, no sign-up, no contact forms, and no advertising. It sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or tracking scripts.

2. Chain reads

Live counters on the Site (current edition, copies printed, claim totals, pool balance) are read from public BNB Chain RPC endpoints directly from your browser. Those RPC providers receive your IP address and the public contract addresses being read, under their own privacy policies. The Site never receives or stores your wallet address: boards read aggregate contract state only.

3. Third party requests

Your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address under Google's privacy policy. Links to BscScan, PancakeSwap, and Uniswap leave this Site and are governed by those services' own policies.

4. Agents and wallets

Printing and claiming happen between your own agent, wallet, and the blockchain. The Site is not a party to those interactions and receives no record of them beyond what is public on chain.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Continued use of the Site after an update means you accept the updated policy.
